# MedTwin — Medical Equipment Digital Twin Platform
🌐 **[Open MedTwin Platform](https://med-twin-aka21329h-med-twin-development.vercel.app)**


MedTwin is a cloud-based SaaS platform for medical equipment management, digital twins, preventive maintenance, service operations, and healthcare asset lifecycle tracking.

## Features

- Medical Equipment Management
- Digital Twins for Medical Devices
- Preventive and Corrective Maintenance
- Work Order Management
- Multi-Facility Management
- Equipment Documentation
- Warranty and Service History
- Equipment Lifecycle Tracking
- Operational Analytics
- AI-Assisted Equipment Support (Planned)

## Technology

Next.js, TypeScript, React, Supabase, PostgreSQL, and cloud infrastructure.

## Live Application

MedTwin is designed to let healthcare organizations manage their medical technology through a centralized web application.
